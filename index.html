From textile thaan packaging to bakery boxes and stationery — we deliver premium-quality, customised packaging materials across India with three decades of manufacturing excellence.

Sharma Packaging Solutions represents the evolution of two trusted businesses: Gurudev Traders and Sarika Chemical, originally led by proprietors,
Suresh Sharma and Vijay Sharma. Backed by this strong foundation, the next generation—Sumit Sharma and Ronak Sharma—have collaborated to launch this specialized packaging enterprise. Operating in close synergy with Sarika Chemical, we provide a seamless, end-to-end packaging ecosystem. This collaborative partnership unites decades of shared industry expertise and vastly expands our manufacturing and production capabilities to serve you better.
